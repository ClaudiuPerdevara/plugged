## Alternative Platforms — Image Search

On these platforms, you can upload pictures of the items you are looking for and get similar results from Chinese suppliers (Taobao, 1688, Weidian, etc.). Ideal for finding specific models at lower prices.

| Platform | Image Search Link | Note |
|----------|-------------------|------|
| **KakoBuy** | https://www.kakobuy.com/search | Shopping agent from China; supports image search to find similar products on Taobao/1688/Weidian |
| **Joyagoo** | https://joyagoo.com/search?searchType=imageSearch | Direct image search; insert the picture of the item and get similar results |
| **Superbuy** | https://www.superbuy.com/en/page/search/ | Popular Chinese shopping agent that also offers image search functionality for sourcing |

**How it works:** Upload a picture of the desired Y2K item → the platform searches for visually similar products → you receive links from Chinese suppliers → you order through the agent (KakoBuy/Joyagoo/Superbuy).
